# Corporate Finance & Financial Analysis

## Overview

This repository presents a portfolio of financial analysis, valuation, accounting, and corporate finance projects developed through graduate study at Thunderbird School of Global Management.

The portfolio demonstrates the application of financial frameworks and Excel based modeling to corporate decision making, including financial performance analysis, discounted cash flow valuation, capital budgeting, equity and credit analysis, cost of capital estimation, and accounting analysis.

## Portfolio Areas

### 1. Accounting & Valuation

#### Liberty Media & Formula 1: Intangible Asset Analysis

Analysis of Liberty Media's acquisition of Formula 1 with emphasis on the accounting and valuation of intangible assets.

The project evaluates the treatment of Formula 1's commercial rights, acquisition accounting, amortization, cash generation, and implications for investors.

**Skills Applied**

• Intangible Asset Analysis  
• Acquisition Accounting  
• Financial Reporting  
• Cash Flow Interpretation  
• Accounting Judgment  
• Investor Analysis

### 7. DuPont Analysis

#### Financial Cockpit

Comparative financial performance analysis examining how companies generate shareholder returns through profitability, asset efficiency, and financial leverage.

The analysis applies the DuPont framework to distinguish between different operating and financing drivers of return on equity.

**Framework**

ROE = Return on Sales × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage

**Skills Applied**

• DuPont Analysis  
• Return on Equity Analysis  
• Profitability Analysis  
• Asset Efficiency  
• Financial Leverage  
• Peer Benchmarking  
• Financial Statement Interpretation  

**File:** `financial-statement-analysis/Financial-Cockpit-DuPont-Analysis.pdf`
